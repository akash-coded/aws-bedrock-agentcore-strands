## Q4. Motion

Remove: sketch parts fading in; the funnel drawing in (blank for its first seconds: `r7/sceptic/funnel-0.6s.jpg`, `base/home-dark-1440-02`); the rolling simulator pictures; the spring curve; the top-bar shape change between pages (keep the change of content, drop the animation); the hero entrance.
