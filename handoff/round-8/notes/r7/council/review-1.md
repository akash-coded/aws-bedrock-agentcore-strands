Paths are under `SP/r7/`. My own prototype frames are in `rev1/`.

## Part 1. The answers

**Strongest: A.** Only A could be built without a question: sizes, timings, the still frame, the phone radius. It also found the one idea that gives the shape change a meaning (an outline until the sign-off, solid after), and the prototype proves it right. It cuts hard and keeps every owner request.

**Biggest blind spot: B.** It drops both hero requests the owner made last round, the changing aircraft and the visible climb, and never says so. Its reason ("will not be seen at comet size") was a guess from the old broken hero. In `p3/home-dark-1440-t0`, `t4` and `t5` the dart, the airliner and the jet each read at once at about 48px. E makes the same call but at least puts it to the owner.

**What all five missed:**

1. The owner said "worse than before". Nobody opened the earlier hero to see what he liked in it.
2. The empty quarter. For about eight seconds of every lap the aircraft is behind the Earth, nothing flies and no phase is lit (`p3/home-dark-1440-t6`). A measured this in the old hero, then wrote the same gap into its own spec.
3. Nobody gave the owner a way to say yes. He has never seen the sketches drawn correctly. Before 20 to 50 of them vanish he should get one page: keep, fix, cut.

## Part 2. Verdicts

1. **Cut the named weak ones. About 75 survive. No quota.** A quota throws out good drawings (`what-is-aidd` has three that read cold) in a style the owner supplied. Cut every sketch two or more advisors named (about 20); where two lessons draw one idea, keep the one in which the worker handles the object.
2. **Keep it, swapped for `the-needle-never-shakes`.** He asked for the home illustration to be fixed, that sketch reads cold, and it comes from lesson one, where the button under it leads.
3. **Keep the change as the prototype has it.** He asked for it, all three shapes are clean in `t0` to `t5`, and the fill at the sign-off is the best moment in the picture.
4. **Three coils, with colour and the tick on the live coil only.** Two rings read as a pair of rings and three fading ones read as a climb, which he asked to see. Grey older coils remove eight coloured segments and the second tick (`t0`).
5. **Keep 75 seconds.** His complaint was "very slowly", and the ground moves the same way as the aircraft at about half its speed, so it reads as flight over a turning Earth.
6. **Remove the reveal.** The board is the content of the page, and a 2.6 second watchdog admits a reader can be left looking at blanks.
7. **Remove view transitions entirely.** A fade between two pages of text lays one on the other, differs in each browser, and answers nothing the reader did.
8. **Relabel, and leave the funnel where it is.** He asked for it there, and it says what the table does not (four methods, one loop). Put "Four methods you may know" over the names and replace "Skip one, and you hear" with a plain sentence.
9. **Move it under the picture.** It carries the licence and the owner's name. That alone does not fit the globe: see 10.
10. **Changes to the prototype:**
    1. **Phone.** At 390 the globe is still cut by the first screen (`rev1/home-dark-390-t0`), the pause button hides the jet (`rev1/home-dark-390-t3`), and the screen edge cuts the jet (`rev1/p390-scrolled-18s`). Make the globe about 220px with the coils inside the gutters, and put pause at the end of the phase line.
    2. **The empty quarter.** Cut the time behind the Earth to about four seconds, and light "Frame" while the aircraft is on its way back, so a phase is always lit.
    3. **The foot of the hero at 1440.** The phase line sits 56px below the counts line, and it and the rings run 20 to 35px past the page's right edge (`t0`). Put both lines on one baseline and end them at the grid.
