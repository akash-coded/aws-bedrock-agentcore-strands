# Advisor: the illustrator and art director

My own shots are in `SP/r7/artdirector/`.

## Q1. Sketches: keep and triage. Refuse model-made pictures. Take the sample off the home page.

Seen correctly (`SP/sk/rev-1.jpg` to `rev-9.jpg`), all 98 hold one style: same worker, ink weight, note colours and blue robot. That is the hard part, and Stability models would lose it: 98 different blobs, no exact words, no theme. Removing all throws away about sixty drawings that teach.

Against the references (`01-two-breakpoints.png`, `08-trust-bridge.png`), six faults recur:

1. **The worker presents, it does not work.** In the references the body bends and both hands are on the thing. Here, in about seventy sketches, the same upright egg touches a box with one stick arm.
2. **No ground.** The references have cliffs, planks, hatching. Here every scene is one flat line with the same two grass ticks.
3. **Notes too long, too many.** The references use one or two words a note. Here notes are lists ("add: a pass mark, a limit on actions, a watch for drift") or four numbers.
4. **Three tellings of one sentence.** Lesson `p0-frame`, 1440 dark (`lesson-dark-1440-04`, `-05`): the quote gives 38 minutes, 240 a day, $9.40; the sketch repeats them; so does the caption.
5. **One idea, many metaphors.** "A rule in the prompt is not a cap in code" is drawn six times (ladder, valve, rope, bag sizer, fuses, chips). Draw an idea once and reuse it.
6. **Diagrams dressed as sketches:** boxes, arrows and words in a handwriting font.

Placement: in dark theme the cream sheet is the brightest, largest thing on the lesson page, 760px wide against text at 632 and the flow map at 944 (`lesson-dark-1440-03`). Set it to the text width, cap the height near 300px, dim the paper.

Cut 17, fix 15 (lists below). About 80 survive if the fixes land, 66 if not. The test: cover the caption; if the drawing alone shows no mechanism, it goes. Notes: three at most, four words each, two numbers.

Home page: remove the sample (`home-dark-1440-07`). The page already has three picture languages (dotted globe, line diagrams, pixel art). A cream cartoon is a fourth.

## Q2. The hero

One canvas, one clock: yes. Today the ribbon is a flat sticker that never goes behind the globe (`hero/home-dark-1440-t2.jpg`). Five changes:

- **Two coils, not three:** the live one and a faint ghost. Three rings round a ball is an atom logo.
- **One hue at a time.** Today six colours show together (t2). Make the path a neutral hairline; only the trail takes its phase hue.
- **No shape change.** At comet size nobody can tell a dart from an airliner, and a shape that only reads at 90px gives the clip-art jet of `t5`. The dart to jet idea already lives in section 2 as four icons (`artdirector/funnel-2100.jpg`). Use the logo's dart and let the trail lengthen phase by phase. This reverses an owner request, so put it to the owner as a choice.
- **No waypoint dots, no "the next round" label** (clipped at t2). One tick for the sign-off; the aircraft slows there.
- **No drag.** Refuse it. On a phone the globe sits where the thumb scrolls, and it adds a second state to the part that was buggy. Fluid comes from depth and easing.

Keep the quiet line under the globe; a cold reader needs it. Phone: a whole globe about 220px, never a cut ball (`hero/home-dark-390-t1.jpg`).
