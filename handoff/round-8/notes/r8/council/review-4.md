## Part 1

**Biggest blind spot: B.** The most exact pictures, no check on any; the labs ship last, after three redesigns. The owner asked for simulations most.

**All five missed:**

1. A recording belongs to one exact prompt. Prompts built from parts multiply the runs (two parts of three options is nine). 4. Walkthrough steps hang on CSS selectors and vanish silently when a page is rebuilt.
