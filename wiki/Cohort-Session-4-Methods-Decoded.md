# Session 4 · Methods decoded

*Where do AI-DLC, AIDD, BMAD and spec-driven development actually sit, and how much process does one change need?*

Part of the [Cohort Kit](Cohort-Kit). Ninety minutes. The session that ends the method wars: by the
end the room can place any named method on the spine, has mapped its own current practice onto it,
and has a rule for how deep to go on a change.

**Pre-reading** (about 35 minutes; the four method lessons are optional depth)

- [AI-DLC vs AIDD vs agentic SDLC](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/ai-dlc-vs-aidd-vs-agentic-sdlc/) — the comparison, and why they are not competitors
- [One lifecycle for every method](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/one-lifecycle-for-every-method/) — how each method occupies part of the same lifecycle
- [How much process a change needs](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/how-much-process-does-a-change-need/) — depth of change as the decision, not method choice
- Optional, one each, split across the room: [What is AI-DLC?](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/what-is-ai-dlc/) · [What is AIDD?](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/what-is-aidd/) · [What is the BMAD Method?](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/what-is-the-bmad-method/) · [What is spec-driven development?](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/what-is-spec-driven-development/)

**Bring.** One page describing how the team delivers today: the ceremonies, the artefacts, the
approvals. It does not need a name.

**You will leave with.** The team's current practice placed on the spine, the parts of any named
method it will borrow, and a written rule for depth of change.

## Facilitator notes

- **This session has no arithmetic and can drift into opinion.** The spine is the anchor: every
  claim about a method has to be placed on it. "AI-DLC is better" is not placeable; "AI-DLC's
  mob elaboration is our P1 spec session" is.
- **The lineage table matters.** Some of what circulates about these methods is vendor material.
  The frameworks page marks confidence per framework; show the room how to read the mark.
- **Depth of change is the takeaway.** A team that leaves with a three-tier rule and examples has
  the whole session. A team that leaves having chosen a method has the wrong thing.
- **Ten-minute version:** the four-methods picture, then straight to the depth-of-change rule.

## Homework

**One page: "how we deliver"**, the team's practice on the spine with the borrowed parts marked,
and the depth-of-change rule at the bottom. This becomes the first page of the team's own operating
guide; the [cadence lesson](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-delivery-cadence/)
in session 8 adds the rhythm.
