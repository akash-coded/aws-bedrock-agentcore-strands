# Session 8 · Practice and roles

*What does each person in the room do on Monday?*

Part of the [Cohort Kit](Cohort-Kit). Ninety minutes. The last session turns seven weeks of method
into one week of work: by the end every attendee has walked their own role's steps, the room has
agreed who owns which artefact, and the candidate project has a first-cycle plan with a rhythm.

**Pre-reading** (about 30 minutes; each attendee reads their own role, then the two shared lessons)

- **Your role:** [product managers](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-pdlc-for-product-managers/) · [program managers](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-pdlc-for-program-managers/) · [solution architects](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-pdlc-for-solution-architects/) · [engineers](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-pdlc-for-engineers/) · [forward-deployed engineers](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/ai-dlc-for-forward-deployed-engineers/) · [QA](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-pdlc-for-qa/) · [DevOps and platform](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-pdlc-for-devops/) · [business sponsors](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-pdlc-for-business-sponsors/) · [executives](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-ai-for-executives/)
- [The SkyWays case study](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/skyways-case-study/) — the ninety days the playbook plays, as prose
- [The operating rhythm](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/agentic-delivery-cadence/) — daily, weekly, quarterly: what the cadence adds to the loops
- Optional, for anyone interviewing or hiring: [Six answer frameworks](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/how-to-answer-ai-interview-questions/) and the question banks in the [Interviews and careers](https://akash-coded.github.io/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/learn/interviews/) track

**Bring.** Everything the cohort has produced: the AI-fit verdict, the bar, the spec, the bolt plan,
the review policy, the golden-set plan, the gate map and the two-number report. Seven artefacts,
however rough. And the "how we deliver" page from session 4.

**You will leave with.** A first-cycle plan for the candidate with an owner per artefact, the
team's cadence written down, and a date for the first gate.

## Facilitator notes

- **Ownership is by name.** "Product owns the bar" is a role sentence and evaporates. "Priya owns the
  bar and its golden set" survives the week. Insist on names for all seven artefacts.
- **The cross-role trade is the best fifteen minutes of the cohort.** People discover that the
  artefact they were waiting for is one they were supposed to produce. Let that run long if it is
  running.
- **The interview questions are not a test.** F1 to F5 round the room shows everyone that they can
  now answer "how accurate does it need to be?" with arithmetic. That is the cohort's proof of its
  own bar.
- **If the team is about to start the real project,** the first gate date is the cohort's last
  artefact. Put it in a calendar before the room empties.
- **Ten-minute version:** the process page role by role, then the first-cycle plan with names.

## Homework

**A [field note](Field-Notes)**, by the facilitator, within a week: what landed, what did not, what
was cut, and what the sessions should say differently. The kit is designed and not yet field-tested,
and the note is how it gets better. Attendees: the first gate, on the date agreed.
