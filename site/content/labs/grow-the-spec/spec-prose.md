Rebooking assistant: what to build

The assistant should offer alternative flights quickly, ideally within about 30 seconds, and should rebook same-day changes when the passenger accepts. For partner flights it should, where possible, give the contact centre a chance to stop the change before it goes through. Refunds may be offered if no flight works, though anything over $400 needs sign-off. If it is unsure it should hand over to an agent as appropriate, and we will need a record of what it did.
