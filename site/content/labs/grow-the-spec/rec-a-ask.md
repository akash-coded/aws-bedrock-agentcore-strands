Eight questions, most blocking first:

1. Which reservation and ticketing systems will the assistant read and write, and is there an API today that lets it change a booking? *Owner: solution architect*
2. What may it change without a human (cabin, fare difference, seats and bags), and what ranks one flight as "best"? *Owner: revenue management, as keeper of the rebooking policy*
3. Where does the passenger meet it (app, SMS, web chat, kiosk), and how does it confirm who they are before changing a booking? *Owner: product owner (Priya)*
4. What exactly triggers a handover, and what does the agent see when the case arrives? *Owner: contact-centre operations lead*
5. Which partner airlines are in scope, and on a partner-operated flight may it make the change or only offer and hand over? *Owner: alliances or partnerships manager*
6. What is the refund cap in dollars, and what is the test for "no sensible flight"? *Owner: finance*
7. What must be logged for each decision, for how long, and which passenger-rights rules must every offer respect? *Owner: compliance*
8. What are the target numbers for "much faster", "most cases" and "rare", and what counts as a mistake? *Owner: product owner (Priya)*

Questions 6 and 7 are the two the page already lists as open. The other six are not on the page at all.
